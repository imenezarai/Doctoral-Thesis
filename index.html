PROPOSED DOCTORAL CONTRIBUTION

What I would investigate

SME sustainability assessment

Digital support for structuring, documenting and managing sustainability topics—an established direction in DiNaOpt4KMU / WILMA.

01
Prediction as a complementary layer

Forecast selected energy and emissions KPIs, and connect predicted deviations to business-process decisions.

Conventional forecasting workflows

Data are cleaned or imputed, then a model produces a prediction. Reliability under changing data quality still needs careful testing.

02
Quality-aware predictive learning

Research models that incorporate quality indicators into forecasts, uncertainty estimates and warning thresholds.

Explanations of model outputs

Feature-attribution tools can show which variables are associated with a forecast.

03
Explanations with reliability context

Evaluate whether explanations remain stable when measurements are missing, delayed or inconsistent.

Business monitoring and reporting

Managers see KPIs and can review performance using enterprise information systems.

04
Closed-loop decision support

Quality warning → forecast → explanation → human action → monitored process outcome.
